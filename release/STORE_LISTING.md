## Full description

A crowded road. One clear way out. Can you untangle the traffic?

Freeway Escape is a traffic puzzle game where every vehicle has a path to freedom. Tap cars, trucks and buses to send them forward, clear space and find the right order to empty the board.

100 PUZZLES, READY TO PLAY
Start with compact 6x6 boards, then take on larger 7x7 and 8x8 layouts. The first 50 levels are available from the start; completing them opens the next 50. All 100 campaign levels are included for offline play.

THINK AHEAD, THEN MAKE YOUR MOVE
Study the traffic, plan an exit and test your solution. Use a hint or undo when you need a hand, or restart a puzzle for free.

MAKE EVERY RETURN COUNT
Collect daily coin gifts, build your streak and earn one-time achievement rewards. Spend game coins on helpful actions, vehicle cosmetics and ad-free passes. View owned skins in Inventory and recent coin activity in Coin History.

A BRIGHT CITY ESCAPE
Enjoy colorful vehicles, cartoon sound effects and background music. Adjust music, sound and haptics in Settings.

KEEP YOUR PROGRESS
Play without connecting a Google account, or connect Google for cloud-save recovery across supported Android installations. Internet access is needed for sign-in, cloud synchronization and ads. Account deletion is available inside the app and through the public support page.

Optional rewarded ads offer coins or progress toward a 24-hour ad-free pass. Automatic ads may appear between completed rounds. Ad-free passes remove automatic level ads; optional rewarded offers remain available. No real-money purchases are enabled in this release.
