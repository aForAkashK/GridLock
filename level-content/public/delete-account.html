Request account deletion

Delete your Freeway Escape account directly in the app, or request deletion by email if you no longer have access to it.

Deletion is permanent

Your cloud progress, coins, Daily and Awards records, owned skins and ad-free pass records cannot be recovered after deletion. This deletes your game account, not your Google account.

Delete directly in the app

  1. Open Freeway Escape → Settings → Save your progress.
  2. Tap Delete account, read the permanent-deletion warning and choose Delete permanently.
  3. If your game is linked to Google, verify the same Google account when prompted. This does not delete your Google account.
  4. Keep your internet connection on until the app shows Account deleted. If interrupted, reopen the app and choose Retry deletion.

The app removes your cloud save and Firebase player account, then clears this device’s local game progress, history and settings. Once deletion has started, retries finish the same operation; the save is not uploaded while deletion is pending.

Only a minimal security marker—your deleted player identifier and deletion time—is retained to stop old signed-in devices from recreating the deleted save. It contains no email, coins, levels or inventory. Your game account and playable cloud save are removed.

I no longer have the app

Email akssoft.feedback@gmail.com from your linked Google email, with subject Freeway Escape account deletion. Include your Player ID if you saved it. It is optional if you no longer have access to it.

For an anonymous save, tell us what information you still have. We may need additional verification to identify it safely. Never send passwords, sign-in tokens or payment details.

Email deletion request

This opens a draft in your email app. Send the email to submit your request. Opening this page or the draft does not submit a request or delete data. If you have no email app, use webmail and send to the address above.

What happens to email requests?

  1. AksSoft reviews your request and verifies ownership. We may ask you to confirm through the linked email before deleting anything.
  2. After verification, we delete the Firebase Authentication player account and associated cloud-save document.
  3. We reply with confirmation of completion and information about any data that must be retained for an applicable legal or security reason.

Email requests are manually processed; in-app deletion is handled directly by the app. Contact the same email for help or request status.

Data covered by deletion

The game account identifier and associated cloud records: coin balance, completed/current levels, Daily claim dates and streaks, Awards/perfect-solve records, owned and equipped skins, reward receipts, ad counters and ad-free pass progress/expiry, plus synchronization metadata.

Data on your device

In-app deletion clears local settings, coin history and offline saves on that device. For email requests or other installed devices, clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall it to remove local records. Copies on other devices cannot be remotely erased by this app. Continuing with a new game creates a new anonymous account.

Other providers and retention

This request does not delete your Google account or separately held Google advertising records. Provider-managed logs or backups follow the relevant provider's retention processes and may not disappear immediately. AksSoft uses request correspondence to verify and process deletion; ask the same support email about any retained information. We do not promise deletion of records we do not control.
