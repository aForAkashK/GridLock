Levels and chapters

The first release includes 100 offline levels. Levels 1–50 are shown first. Completing the first chapter reveals 51–100; levels within each chapter unlock in order. A paid Skip advances progress.

Restarting and assistance

Restarting a puzzle is always free. Hints, Undo and Skip use earned coins. Completed levels can be replayed, but do not pay a second completion reward.

Daily coins

Open Daily from Home to collect once per local calendar day. Consecutive days pay 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 80, then 100 coins. Day 7 onward stays at 100. Miss a day and the next claim resets to Day 1. New rewards unlock at your device’s local midnight. Claims work offline; check your device date and time if a reward is paused.

Awards

There are 10 achievements: five level-completion milestones, three perfect-solve goals and two daily-claim streak goals. Collect each coin bonus once from Awards on Home. Level milestones count existing completions, including skips. Perfect goals track different levels solved at par or better from the Awards update onward; replay old levels to make them count. Daily goals remember your best recorded claim streak, even after it resets.

Keeping your save

Connect Google from Settings before uninstalling or switching devices. On a new device, connect the same account and confirm restoring its save. Completed levels are combined and the existing cloud coin balance is used; two balances are not added together. Anonymous sign-in alone is not a reliable reinstall backup.

Shop and Inventory

Red Racer costs 300 earned coins. Preview it in Shop, confirm the purchase, then equip it from Inventory. Classic Garage stays free. The cosmetic changes red compact cars only. Coin History shows the latest 200 entries on this device from the update onward; older activity and history from other devices are unavailable. Owned skins sync with your save. No real-money purchases are enabled.

Ads and ad-free passes

Open Coins & Ad-Free from the coin + panel or Settings. Coin ads award 10, 15, 20, 25, 35 and 50 coins for the first six completed ads each local day, then 10 coins each. There is no daily claim limit. A separate set of five completed pass ads unlocks 24 hours without automatic level ads; those views earn no coins. Partial pass progress is kept. Seven days cost 1,000 earned coins; 30 days cost 3,000. Coin passes extend an existing expiry. Optional rewarded ads remain available while ad-free. Closing an ad early does not grant its reward. If no level ad is ready, the next level opens immediately. Review ad privacy choices in Settings.

Account deletion

Delete your game account and cloud save through Settings → Save your progress → Delete account. If you no longer have the app, use our account-deletion page. It works with or without the app.

Report a problem

Please include your device model, game version, level number and a description or screenshot of the problem. For cloud-save issues include your Player ID from Settings. Do not include credentials or tokens.

Privacy information

Report an app issue

Open Settings → Report a problem to review and share app/device diagnostics. Email the report to akssoft.feedback@gmail.com with steps to reproduce the issue, the level, and an optional screenshot. Your Support code helps us match crash reports; your Player ID is needed only for cloud-save/account requests. Do not send passwords or Google tokens.
