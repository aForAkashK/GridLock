Privacy policy

How Freeway Escape handles your game save, account and advertising information.

Developed by AksSoft · Updated 30 October 2026
Developer portfolio

Your save

The game saves coins, completed levels, your current level, owned/equipped skins, settings and the latest 200 coin-history entries on your device. The bundled 100-level campaign can be played offline.

Cloud saves and sign-in

Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous player identifier. If you choose to connect Google, Google and Firebase process the sign-in and associated account information. The game uses the account identifier to recover your save. Cloud save records contain coins, completed level IDs, current level, the daily-reward claim date/time and streak, perfect-solve level IDs, best recorded daily streak, collected achievement-reward IDs, owned skin IDs, the equipped skin, rewarded-ad daily counters, ad-free-pass progress and expiry, reward receipt IDs, a synchronization version, the last update reason and update time.

Connecting Google is optional. Anonymous progress may not be recoverable after an Android uninstall. Uninstalling removes local game data but does not automatically delete a linked cloud save.

Detailed coin history stays on the device and is not included in cloud saves. Uninstalling removes this local history.

Online connections

The app contacts Firebase for authentication, cloud save synchronization and optional future level downloads. Hosting and authentication providers may process connection information such as IP addresses as part of operating their services. See Google’s privacy policy.

This release

This version includes Google AdMob rewarded ads and ads at level transitions. There are no real-money purchases or a separate gameplay analytics SDK. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts or location access. Sound and vibration preferences can be changed in Settings.

Crash reports and issue diagnostics

Firebase Crashlytics helps us investigate crashes and handled technical errors. In production builds, automatic crash reporting is on by default and can be turned off in Settings → Automatic crash reports. Reports may include exception stack traces, app/build and operating-system versions, device characteristics, screen/display settings, the current level and move count, sound settings, and a limited trail of recent game actions. We attach a random support code rather than your Firebase player identifier or email. We do not attach login tokens, your cloud-save document, screenshots or full coin history. Crashlytics also processes its own installation/session identifiers and diagnostic data; see Firebase privacy information.

Settings → Report a problem creates a report you can review and share yourself. It contains the support code, app/device context and up to 50 recent actions from the current session. This local history is kept in memory, not synchronized as part of your save. Turning automatic reporting off does not prevent you from sharing a report voluntarily. Deleting your game account clears this device’s app-assigned Support code and requests deletion of unsent crash reports; reports already submitted are handled separately under the provider’s retention controls. Contact akssoft.feedback@gmail.com with your support code for diagnostic-data questions or deletion requests. Crash reporting cannot identify every visual, sound or connection problem; a description and optional screenshot remain helpful.

Advertising and privacy choices

Google Mobile Ads may process IP addresses, device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Non-personalized ads are requested; this does not eliminate SDK data processing. Where required, Google’s consent flow is shown before ad requests. You can review available choices under Settings → Ad privacy choices. Optional rewarded ads grant coins or progress toward an ad-free pass only after completion. An ad-free pass removes automatic level ads while optional rewarded ads remain available. See Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

Control and support

You can remove on-device data through your device settings. To ask about your data, request a cloud-save deletion, or report a problem, email akssoft.feedback@gmail.com or visit the developer portfolio. Include the Player ID shown in game Settings when the request concerns a cloud save. Never send passwords, Google tokens or signing keys.

Account and data deletion

Delete your game account and associated cloud save directly through Settings → Save your progress → Delete account, or use the public account-deletion page without the app. In-app deletion verifies ownership, removes the player cloud save and Firebase account, and clears local game data on that device. A minimal marker containing the deleted player identifier and deletion time is retained solely to prevent stale sessions from recreating deleted saves; it contains no email or gameplay data. The public page explains retries, email requests, ownership verification and handling of local/provider-managed data. Uninstalling alone does not delete your cloud account.
